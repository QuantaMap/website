[QuantaMap](https://www.quantamap.eu) and  [TNO](https://www.tno.nl) kick off a project to improve the quality of spin qubits.  

In our collaborative [MKB Hightech project from Holland High Tech](https://hollandhightech.nl/nieuws-agenda/nieuws/13-mkb-ers-aan-de-slag-met-innovatie-na-toekenning-in-de-mkb-hightech-call-2024) we will develop the tools necessary to improve current designs of spin qubit chips. 

Within the QUACIMI project (QUAntitative, Cryogenic Imaging of MIcromagnets on Quantum Chips) we will   focus on developing an advanced quantitative imaging technique and the hard and software required for it. The goal is to characterize the magnetic fields of on-chip micromagnets, which are crucial for this type of scalable quantum computer based on spin qubits.
