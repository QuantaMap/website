🚀 Big news from QuantaMap: 
We have been awarded the prestigious EIC - European Innovation Council Accelerator Grant of €2.5 million, combined with an equity investment component by the EIB.
This will accelerate the commercialization of our SQUID-AFM tool for quantum R&D and the development as an in-line quality control platform for quantum chip manufacturing.
Our technology provides unique insights that will help the quantum industry to transition from lab to fab, with better chip performance, production consistency and yield. 

The EIC funding also provides us with bandwidth to serve some more launching customers.
